Hasmadi Textile: E-commerce Website Development

During my time at Hasmadi Textile, I independently built and managed the company's e-commerce website using WordPress. The platform was developed to establish an online presence and give customers a digital channel to explore and purchase school uniforms, school-related attire and textile products.

The challenge

The business needed an online platform to showcase its product range and support its digital sales activities. This involved setting up the website infrastructure and bringing together the different elements required to operate an e-commerce platform.

My approach

I handled most of the website development and setup independently, covering the key areas needed to establish the online store. My responsibilities included:

  • Website setup and development using WordPress
  • Domain and hosting configuration
  • E-commerce setup and product presentation
  • Payment gateway integration
  • Website management and maintenance

Product categories

The website was intended to showcase products across several categories:

  • School uniforms and school-related clothing
  • Other school attire and apparel
  • Fabric and textile products

Project outcome

The project established an online sales platform for Hasmadi Textile, giving the business an additional digital channel to present its products and reach customers.

The website is no longer active following the end of its hosting arrangement. Original website screenshots and design files are also unavailable, so this case study documents my responsibilities and project experience rather than reproducing the original website interface.
