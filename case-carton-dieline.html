Automating Carton Dieline Creation

An internal Adobe Illustrator automation tool that creates corrugated shipping box dielines from user-defined carton dimensions.

The challenge

Creating carton dielines manually took at least 10 minutes per design. Repetitive measurements and layout adjustments made the process time-consuming.

The solution

I developed an Adobe Illustrator tool that generates complete carton dielines automatically. Users enter the width, length and height, select a carton colour, and generate a ready-to-use dieline directly in Illustrator.

Development process

The tool was developed using JavaScript / ExtendScript, with AI-assisted coding to support implementation. A key development consideration was ensuring accurate overall dieline dimensions and spacing based on the specified carton measurements.

Results and impact

10+ minManual dieline preparation (at least)
< 1 minWith the tool
90%+Time reduction per comparable task
  • Reduced dieline preparation time from at least 10 minutes to under 1 minute.
  • Achieved over 90% time reduction per comparable task.
  • Automated complete dieline generation for corrugated shipping boxes.
  • Regularly used in actual packaging production work.

Project outcome

An in-house automation solution that simplifies carton template creation and improves efficiency in the packaging design workflow.
